{# How to cite the technote, at the end of the article: the full bibliographic citation, with the DOI written as a resolvable link. A technote registered with a DOI is that DOI's landing page, and DataCite asks a landing page to show a complete citation a reader can copy, not just the identifier. The sidebar's Cite section (components/sidebar-citation.html) offers the DOI and the BibTeX entry for a reference manager; this is the human-readable sentence, placed where a reader arrives once they have finished reading. The theme's own sections/article-footer.html is intentionally empty and is overridden here, which is the seam the theme provides for end matter. The text and the link both come from the html_context that documenteer.conf.technote publishes (documenteer.citations composes them), pre-split at the DOI so that this template hyperlinks it without doing string surgery of its own: writing the lead and then the link reproduces the citation exactly. A technote without a DOI leaves documenteer_technote_citation unset, so nothing at all is rendered -- the article footer stays as empty as the theme leaves it. Every interpolated value is escaped explicitly, because Sphinx's Jinja environment does not autoescape and a citation is composed from free text in technote.toml -- a title with an ampersand in it is ordinary. #} {%- if documenteer_technote_citation and documenteer_technote_citation.doi_url %}
