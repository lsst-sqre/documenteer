A technote registered with a DOI is that DOI's landing page, and DataCite asks a landing page to show the DOI as a resolvable link together with a bibliographic record. Both values come from the html_context that documenteer.conf.technote publishes (documenteer.citations composes them); this template composes nothing. A technote without a DOI leaves documenteer_technote_citation unset, so nothing at all is rendered -- not even an empty section. Every interpolated value is escaped explicitly, because Sphinx's Jinja environment does not autoescape and a citation is composed from free text in technote.toml -- a title with an ampersand in it is ordinary. #} {%- if documenteer_technote_citation and documenteer_technote_citation.doi_url %}
