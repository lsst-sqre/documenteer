### New features

- A user guide that declares `[[project.citations]]` now emits machine-readable DOI landing-page metadata in every page's `<head>`, which is what a DOI registration agency, Google Scholar, and Google Dataset Search read. The `self` citation's DOI appears as a Highwire `citation_doi` meta tag (bare) and a Dublin Core `DC.identifier` meta tag (as the resolvable `https://doi.org/` URL), alongside a schema.org JSON-LD block that makes the site the subject of the document: a node identified by its DOI, carrying the title, creators, publisher, publication date, and a `PropertyValue` identifier, with the site's other citations hanging off it as schema.org `citation` values. A citation labelled "Dataset" crosswalks to a schema.org `Dataset`, the type Google Dataset Search indexes, and creators carry resolvable ORCID and ROR identifiers. A guide that declares no citations emits none of this and is unchanged.
