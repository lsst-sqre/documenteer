### Bug fixes

- Editing `[[project.citations]]` in `documenteer.toml` now reaches every citation surface on the build that follows, instead of only on a fresh one. The `citation-card` directive and the `doi` role resolve their entry while a document is *read* and write the rendered result into the doctree cache, so an edit that left every document up to date used to leave those surfaces showing the previous build's citation while the `<head>` metadata, the JSON-LD block, and the footer — all composed as a page is written — showed the edited one. The guide preset now publishes a digest of the resolved citations as the `documenteer_citations_digest` configuration value, whose `rebuild` is `"env"`, so a site that edits a citation re-reads its documents; a site whose citations are unchanged still rebuilds incrementally. The `documenteer.citation_page` warning about a `page` claim's docname or fragment, which Sphinx runs only when a document was re-read, now also reports a claim added or edited in `documenteer.toml` alone.
