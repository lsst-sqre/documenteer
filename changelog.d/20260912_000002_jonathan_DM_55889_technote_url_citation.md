### New features

- Every technote now shows its citation surfaces, not only one registered with a DOI. A technote's URL is a stable locator for the document, so a technote with no DOI is cited by its `canonical_url`: the **Cite** section in the sidebar and the **Citing this document** section at the end of the article are rendered for every technote, the displayed citation ends in that URL, and the BibTeX entry — keyed by the handle, as before — carries a `url` field and no `doi`. This is how lsst-texmf's `lsst.bib` already records a DOI-less technote, so the entry a reader copies from the page matches the one they would have found there.

  Displaying a citation is not claiming a registration, so nothing that needs a DOI has moved: the sidebar's DOI line, the `citation_doi` Highwire tag, `DC.identifier` as a DOI, the JSON-LD `identifier`, and `documenteer technote lint`'s TN105 DataCite cross-check are all still DOI-only, and a technote without one emits none of them. A technote that declares neither a `doi` nor a `canonical_url` has nothing to be located by, and its citation is the same reference ending after the publisher, with nothing hyperlinked and no `url` field in the entry.
