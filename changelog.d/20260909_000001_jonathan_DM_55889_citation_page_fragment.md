### New features

- A user guide's `[[project.citations]]` entry that claims a page with a fragment — `page = "<docname>#<fragment>"` — now has the fragment checked too, not just the docname. A fragment that names no anchor on the page it claims emits a `documenteer.citation_page` warning naming the citation, the fragment, and the docname, and recommending an explicit `.. _label:` target; when the page records explicit targets of its own, the warning offers them. This is the failure that would otherwise reach doi.org: a fragment is usually a heading's generated anchor, so rewording a heading from "TAP" to "TAP service" turns `#tap` into `#tap-service` with a green build, and the URL registered at DataCite for a minted DOI quietly stops resolving to anything on the page. The check runs alongside the existing docname check, is suppressible under the same `documenteer.citation_page` name, and the guide documentation now writes its fragment examples as explicit targets an author controls.
