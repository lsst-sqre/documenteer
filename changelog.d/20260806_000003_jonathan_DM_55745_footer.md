### New features

- The user-guide theme now has a custom Rubin Observatory page footer. It leads with an inventory of project links (Rubin Observatory with "For scientists" and "News" sub-links, Documentation at www.lsst.io, data.lsst.cloud, and the Community forum), followed by the copyright statement, the NSF/DOE funding statement in small print, and the funding-partner logo lineup (which inverts to white in the dark theme). The footer content is aligned with the article column (accounting for the primary sidebar) by a small script, falling back to a centered article-width layout without JavaScript. The footer replaces the theme's default components, so the Sphinx and pydata-sphinx-theme attribution lines no longer appear.
