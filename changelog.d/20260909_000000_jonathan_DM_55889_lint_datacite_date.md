### New features

- `documenteer technote lint`'s **TN105** DataCite cross-check reports the registered issue date to a DOI-bearing technote that declares no `date_updated`. Such a technote is dated by the commit it is published from, and `date_updated` is the field that pins the citation to a fixed day instead — so a finding raised for some other reason, a drifted title or a drifted author list, closes by saying when the record was issued, that the technote is dated by its publishing commit, and that declaring `date_updated` pins the citation to a fixed date. The sentence is never a finding on its own: a technote that matches its registered record and declares no date reports nothing at all. The date comes from `publicationYear`, which DataCite defines as the citation year, with an `Issued`-typed entry of `dates` adding day precision where one is registered; the DOI record's own `created`/`registered`/`updated` timestamps date the *DOI* rather than the technote and are deliberately not consulted. A `date_updated` the technote *does* declare is never compared against the registered date, since an edit legitimately postdates a DOI and a minting legitimately postdates the last edit. TN105 stays silent offline, on a 404, and on an unreadable response exactly as before. See [TN105](https://documenteer.lsst.io/technotes/lint/tn105.html).
