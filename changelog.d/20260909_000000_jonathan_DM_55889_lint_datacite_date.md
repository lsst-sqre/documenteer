### New features

- `documenteer technote lint`'s **TN105** DataCite cross-check now tells a DOI-bearing technote that declares no `date_updated` what date to declare. A technote without one is cited undated, and the registered DOI record is where the missing date already is, so the finding quotes it back: a record that registers a full date quotes the line to write (`set 'date_updated = 2016-05-24'`), and a record that registers only a year — which is every Rubin-minted record, since DataCite's mandatory `publicationYear` is its only date — names the year to look within. The date comes from `publicationYear`, which DataCite defines as the citation year, with an `Issued`-typed entry of `dates` adding day precision where one is registered; the DOI record's own `created`/`registered`/`updated` timestamps date the *DOI* rather than the technote and are deliberately not consulted. A `date_updated` the technote *does* declare is never compared against the registered date, since an edit legitimately postdates a DOI and a minting legitimately postdates the last edit. TN105 stays silent offline, on a 404, and on an unreadable response exactly as before. See [TN105](https://documenteer.lsst.io/technotes/lint/tn105.html).
