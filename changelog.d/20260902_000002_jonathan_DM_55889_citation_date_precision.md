### Other changes

- A `[[project.citations]]` entry's `date` is now kept at the precision its source states, instead of being padded out to a day. Alongside the full TOML date (`date = 2025-06-30`), an entry can write a bare year (`date = 2025`) or a quoted ISO 8601 date (`date = "2025-06"`, `date = "2025"`), and the schema.org `datePublished` a page publishes is `"2025-06-30"`, `"2025"`, or `"2025-06"` accordingly — never a day the configuration did not state. A value that is not one of those forms fails the build with a message naming all three. A `CITATION.cff` file is read the same way: `date-released` and `date-published` date a work to the day, and a reference carrying only `year`, or `year` and `month`, now dates it to the year or the month rather than to the first of January. Plain-text citations, BibTeX keys, and the BibTeX `year` field are unchanged, since a rendered citation shows only the year at every precision.
