### New features

- The **TN105** DataCite cross-check in `documenteer technote lint` now compares a technote's authors against the registered creators as structured identities rather than as reconciled name strings. Each author is paired with a creator by ORCID first — normalized on both sides, so a bare identifier and an `orcid.org` URL agree — and a pair an ORCID settles is never reported for spelling a name differently. Whoever is left over is paired on the decomposed `givenName` and `familyName` DataCite registers: family names fold case, accents, and punctuation, and given names tolerate initials, so a registered `James F.` matches a declared `James` and `R. Lynne` matches `Lynne`. A creator that registers no given name — an `Organizational` creator, or the family-name-only `Personal` creator Rubin's minter registers a committee as — is compared on that one name, which also stops the literal `null` the minter writes into such a record's formatted `name` from being reported as drift. Only the authors left unpaired are reported, and the finding names them on each side. Reading the parts rather than a sorted bag of name tokens also means a transposed given and family name is now reported instead of silently accepted.
