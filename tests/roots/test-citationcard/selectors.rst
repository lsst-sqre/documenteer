Selecting an entry
==================

An entry answers to its label, to its BibTeX key, and to its DOI in any
spelling, on both surfaces.

Card by key
-----------

.. citation-card:: RTN-115

Card by bare DOI
----------------

.. citation-card:: 10.5281/zenodo.10385500

Card by prefixed DOI
--------------------

.. citation-card:: doi:10.5281/zenodo.10385500

Card by DOI URL
---------------

.. citation-card:: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10385500

Role by key
-----------

The report is published as :doi:`RTN-115`.

Role by DOI
-----------

The dataset is published as :doi:`doi:10.5281/zenodo.10385500`.

----------------------------

See :doi:`the dataset release <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10385500>` for
